## Important scope differences

- 1954/674/675 share Apple model, colour and storage intent. 1954 additionally advertises FMI; 675 is serial-only. No automatic paid fallback is configured.
- EID lookup (1956) and eSIM compatibility (1941) are separate outcomes.
- KDDI status (729/731), KDDI finance (730), Docomo finance (732) and SoftBank finance (733) keep the exact carrier and question.
- KG enrollment (700) is not verified KME/MDM. The old KME candidate is unavailable.
- 1990 advertises sales country/date, not proof of the original seller.
- 2015 is Xfinity unlock eligibility, not a finance-balance check.
- 703 advertises 12-character serials; 712 advertises 10-character serial support. 697/698 and HP 724 use machine key IMEI despite serial input.
- MEID, Xiaomi code and PC serial/service-tag entries require compatible forms. ASUS graphics-card warranty also needs a supported hardware category.
- Turnaround, model restrictions and optional additional fields remain variant-specific. Source numbering does not become a new customer benefit.
- Unavailable old candidates (refurbished-only, Mac photo, Rakuten and generic Japan coverage) remain unavailable; no substitute is silently assigned.
