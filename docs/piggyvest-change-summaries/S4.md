# S4: hide goal account numbers and alert on unmapped deposits

## What changes
Savings goals no longer show a bank account number anywhere in the app. The wallet screen no longer looks up or displays a goal account, the plan funding screens no longer keep the number in memory, and the customer is told to fund a plan from their PiggyVest wallet. When a bank deposit arrives for a PiggyVest wallet that matches none of our goals or wallets, an operator alert is raised once per deposit. The deposit is not credited and stays retryable.

## Why
Money sent by bank transfer straight to a goal account does not reach the goal. Hiding the number removes the chance that a customer sends money that way. If a deposit still arrives, an operator needs to know whose money it is.

## What could go wrong and how it is prevented
A customer could still see a goal account number through an old screen. The wallet hero screen that did this is removed, and tests assert that no goal account number is fetched or shown there or in the plan funding screens. An alert could be missed or repeated. It is raised once per deposit per server instance, so repeated deliveries do not page again, and an alert that fails is tried again on the next delivery. A failing alert can never change whether the deposit is retried or credited.

## How it is tested
Tests cover the wallet hero, the wallet content, the plan funding hook and the transfer modal, checking that no goal account number is requested or rendered. Webhook tests cover the alert being raised, not raised for other failures, raised once across redeliveries, and the retry outcome when the alert itself fails.

## How it stays switched off
The change only removes display and adds an alert. It adds no payment path and does not change any production setting. PiggyVest funding stays behind its existing switches, which remain off.
