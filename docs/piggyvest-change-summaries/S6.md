# S6: cancel a savings goal and refund the whole balance to the main wallet

## What changes
A customer can cancel a savings goal and get everything in it, interest included, back in their PiggyVest main wallet. The server hands out a one-off attempt number, the customer types their PIN, and that proof is tied to the goal and attempt. The server asks PiggyVest what the goal holds and moves exactly that. We never work out interest ourselves. Once PiggyVest confirms, one database step marks the goal cancelled and adds the money to the customer's balance in our wallet records, so it can be spent or saved again. A goal under cancel cannot be added to or spent.

## Why
PiggyVest holds the money, so cancelling must be one checkable transfer that cannot happen twice or start from a borrowed PIN proof, and our records must agree with PiggyVest's.

## What could go wrong and how it is prevented
- Double tap or retry: the transfer reference is built from the goal, so PiggyVest cannot move the money twice. Only one cancel per goal can be live.
- Timeout: we never send again. A retry only asks PiggyVest about that reference. A "not found" straight after sending counts as uncertain.
- Customer closes the app: PiggyVest's transfer notification also finds the cancel and finishes it.
- Failed transfer, then retry: the next try gets a numbered reference.
- Late success: if PiggyVest shows the transfer as done only after we released it, a status check or notification finds that attempt again and settles it. A newer live try is flagged for review.
- Money counted twice: an order cannot spend a goal under cancel, and the refund reaches our wallet records exactly once.
- Account deleted mid-cancel: deleting the account or goal waits until the cancel settles.
- Invented attempt number: the PIN check refuses numbers this database did not issue for that goal, and the proof is spent in the step that reserves the attempt.
- Balance above the automatic limit (about 100 million naira): nothing is sent, the reservation is released, and the customer is told to contact support.
- Open point: confirm with PiggyVest in sandbox that numbered references work.

## How it is tested
Database tests cover attempt numbers, numbered retries, late success, wrong PIN bindings, expiry, replays, mismatched settlements, wallet credit, the order, deletion and funding blocks, and permissions. Unit tests cover one-shot sending, timeouts, notifications and schemas. All mocked; no live call was made.

## How it stays switched off
Nothing turns anything on. The mobile app does not call the new route yet, and the complete action is refused until S7. The route needs PiggyVest settings that are not set in production.
