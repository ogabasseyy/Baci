# S6: cancel a savings goal and refund the whole balance to the main wallet

## What changes
A customer can cancel a savings goal and get everything in it, interest included, back in their PiggyVest main wallet. The server hands out a one-off attempt number, the customer types their PIN, and that proof is tied to the goal and attempt. The server asks PiggyVest what the goal holds and moves exactly that. We never work out interest ourselves. Once PiggyVest confirms, one database step marks the goal cancelled and adds the money to the customer's balance in our wallet records. A goal under cancel cannot be added to or spent.

## Why
PiggyVest holds the money, so cancelling must be one checkable transfer that cannot happen twice or start from a borrowed PIN proof, and our records must agree with PiggyVest's.

## What could go wrong and how it is prevented
- Double tap or retry: the transfer reference is built from the goal, so the money cannot move twice. Only one cancel per goal can be live.
- Timeout: we never send again. A retry only asks PiggyVest about that reference.
- Customer closes the app: PiggyVest's notification finishes the cancel.
- Failed transfer, then retry: the next try gets a numbered reference.
- Late success: if PiggyVest shows a transfer as done only after we released it, a status check settles it. If a newer live try blocks that, the customer sees "under review" (never "rejected") and a person is alerted.
- Money counted twice: an order cannot spend a goal under cancel, and the refund reaches our records exactly once.
- Mixed goals: a goal tied to a PiggyVest wallet no longer accepts old-style contributions or order spending, because those change only our records. A goal that already holds either is never refunded; the customer is told it needs review and a person is alerted.
- Invented attempt number: refused; the proof is spent in the step that reserves the attempt.
- Balance above the automatic limit: nothing is sent and the customer is told to contact support.
- Open point: confirm with PiggyVest in sandbox that numbered references work.

## How it is tested
Database tests cover mixed-goal refusals, attempt numbers, numbered retries, late success, wrong PIN bindings, expiry, replays, mismatched settlements, wallet credit, funding blocks and permissions. Unit tests cover one-shot sending, timeouts, alerts, notifications and schemas. All mocked; no live call was made.

## How it stays switched off
Nothing turns anything on. Starting or sending a cancel is refused unless the wallet and savings switches are on and the master kill switch is not pulled. Status checks keep working.
