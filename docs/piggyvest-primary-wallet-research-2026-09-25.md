# PiggyVest-primary wallet research

Research date: 25 September 2026. Read-only review of public PiggyVest Business API documentation and this worktree. This is a design finding, not deployment approval, provider confirmation of commercial terms, or proof of end-to-end sandbox money movement. The wallet-interest use case is already business-approved; the questions below concern its exact technical and financial contract.

## Intended product model

- The customer's everyday balance is backed by a PiggyVest customer default wallet, with a provider funding account. The customer owns the spendable principal, but receives no interest entitlement on this balance. Ogabassey would receive any eligible ordinary-balance interest only if PiggyVest supports and contractually approves routing to an Ogabassey-owned wallet.
- A provider-managed **0% customer / 100% business interest split** could implement that entitlement more directly than payout routing alone, if PiggyVest enables it for these API wallets. An owner-provided dashboard screenshot shows an interest-take percentage field and payout-profile selector; the public create-customer and create-wallet request bodies still do not document a split-percentage parameter. `interest_payout_wallet` names a destination, not a share percentage; `enable_interest_accrual: false` disables accrual rather than assigning the business a 100% share. Do not simulate a split in Baci's ledger before provider-confirmed payout evidence.
- Each opted-in device savings plan has a separate PiggyVest wallet. Only funds confirmed in that plan wallet can create customer savings principal or eligible plan interest. Plan interest pays to the plan/customer destination under confirmed terms; unpaid accrual is not spendable.
- Paystack may remain a separately reconciled card-collection channel. Its existing dedicated bank account numbers cannot be silently represented as PiggyVest wallet accounts or switched while old numbers may still receive deposits.
- The internal Baci ledger remains necessary for merchant/customer ownership, pending movements, liabilities, idempotency, reversals, purchase reservations and reconciliation. It is not an additional pool of cash on top of provider balances.

## Current Baci mismatch

- `apps/mobile-storefront/hooks/wallet-data.ts` reads the everyday balance from `customer_wallets` and only accepts a `paystack` funding account from `customer_wallet_payment_accounts`. `apps/web/src/app/api/storefront/customer/wallet/funding-account/route.ts` provisions the general-wallet Paystack DVA. This is not a PiggyVest-primary everyday wallet.
- `apps/web/src/app/api/storefront/customer/savings/funding/route.ts` and `apps/web/src/lib/piggyvest/customer-plan-funding-ensure.ts` provision PiggyVest only after an active savings goal and BVN. That flow creates the PiggyVest customer/default wallet as a side effect, then the plan wallet; it does not bind the default wallet to the everyday Baci wallet.
- The customer-creation request currently copies the plan's `enableInterestAccrual` value and uses `interestPayout: 'own_wallet'`. Thus a first interest-enabled plan could create the default wallet with accrual but no verified company payout destination; a first non-interest plan could disable default-wallet accrual. Neither encodes the requested company entitlement for ordinary balances. `apps/web/src/lib/piggyvest/provisioning-request.ts` supports a configured payout destination only when a verified wallet ID is supplied, but the customer-facing plan funding path does not use it.
- The existing plan design deliberately assigns one provider wallet per savings plan. That can be retained while replacing only the everyday wallet's provider rail. The current savings interest/cancellation policy and its unresolved paid-interest recovery remain separate gates.
- `docs/piggyvest-contract-register.md` records a provider-message staging funding charge of ₦50 through ₦50,000 and ₦75 above that amount. The payer, production applicability and settlement effect remain unresolved; these are not public API tariff guarantees.

## Recommended sequence, not yet authorized for activation

1. Freeze two independent entitlements: ordinary balance interest, if provider-eligible, belongs to Ogabassey; opted-in plan interest belongs to the customer. Principal never becomes Ogabassey revenue. Record the provider's actual payout destination and paid-interest event/period before ledger recognition.
2. Confirm the provider's default-wallet/API-wallet type and interest eligibility, merchant payout-wallet ownership and inheritance rules. Do not set `enable_interest_accrual: false` to implement merchant interest: it disables accrual on the documented customer path.
3. Design authenticated, consented PiggyVest customer/default-wallet provisioning for customers who supply required BVN. Persist a trusted `(merchant, customer) -> provider customer/default wallet` mapping, separate from `(merchant, customer, goal) -> plan wallet`. No synthetic BVN outside isolated staging fixtures.
4. Build a provider-neutral everyday wallet read/funding adapter with a server-selected active provider, verified account ownership, pending/ready states and an authoritative reconciliation ledger. Keep Paystack and PiggyVest inbound receipts distinct; no client-authoritative balance changes or double credit.
5. Migrate in cohorts only after exact liabilities, old Paystack DVAs, provider settlement ownership and late-deposit routing are reconciled. Stop issuing new Paystack DVAs only after the PiggyVest path passes authenticated sandbox tests. Keep old account numbers monitored until the provider confirms their closure or redirection behavior.
6. For customer-authorized savings allocation, reserve source funds, submit a stable-reference PiggyVest wallet transfer, and recognize the plan contribution only on final confirmed destination credit. HTTP 202 and UI callbacks remain pending. Reconcile duplicates, response loss, fees, reversals and interest eligibility.
7. Prove staging onboarding, account reservation, signed inflow, spend, plan transfer, interest payout, cancellation and restart/replay with synthetic customers and amounts before requesting a separate production cutover decision. No provider write, deployment, DNS, secret change or money movement is authorized by this note.

## Exact provider confirmations needed

1. Does the API customer **default wallet** earn interest when `enable_interest_accrual: true`, even if used as an everyday spending wallet? In the dashboard form, does a `3` take mean three percentage points of the displayed 12% or 3% of earned interest? Is a take equal to the full displayed rate permitted, yielding **0% customer interest** and the full eligible business differential, without disabling accrual or touching principal? Which party receives the “balance,” and which verified business wallet receives the net payout? Is that split enabled for Ogabassey's approved use case?
2. Is the dashboard interest-take setting global across all API wallets? Do later customer-linked plan wallets have independent interest rates/shares and payout destinations, or inherit the customer/default-wallet setting? Can any of these settings be changed after creation for existing wallets, and how are old accrued/paid periods treated?
3. What are the documented interest rates/tiers, tax, rounding, minimum balance, payout period, reversals and forfeiture rules for each wallet type? Does the four-withdrawal counter apply per wallet or customer, and do internal transfers count?
4. What KYC is mandatory to create the default wallet/funding account for an existing Baci customer? Confirm BVN handling, duplicate-customer matching, virtual-account provisioning limits, account closure and late deposits.
5. Do the previously supplied ₦50/₦75 staging bank-funding charges apply to the default wallet and to production? Who pays them, what amount is credited, and what are the wallet-transfer, failed/reversed transaction and settlement rules? The public transaction-fee page configures transfer-fee responsibility; it is not a deposit tariff schedule.
6. What provider-approved migration route can move currently outstanding customer wallet liabilities from the Paystack-backed arrangement without losing late deposits or charging/crediting the customer twice?

No message has been sent to PiggyVest. These questions are for an owner-approved provider follow-up, not a renewed business-use-case approval request.
